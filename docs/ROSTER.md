# Character roster and district population plan

All people are generated by `assets/blender/build_characters.py` (MakeHuman base mesh via MPFB2, era clothing cut
from the body-conforming helpers, rigged, idle/walk/sentry clips). Animals and the dragon by
`assets/blender/build_assets.py -- --animals`. Placement in the square is data: `data/npcs.json`.

## Player origins (man / woman)
figure_noble, figure_artist, figure_veteran, figure_merchant, figure_priest (woman = nun), figure_kazimierz, plus
figure_townsman / figure_townswoman as generic stand-ins. Watchman = Austrian infantry, 1784-pattern musket.

## Townsfolk (buildings and missions)
`TOWNSFOLK` dict: innkeeper, tavern maid, cloth merchant and wife, Kazimierz shopkeeper, baker, blacksmith, apothecary,
tailor, bookseller's clerk, coachman, customs officer, market woman, washerwoman, lamplighter, night watchman,
banker, Sejm politician, councillor, mayor, professor, scholar, judge, advocate, notary, mercenaries.

## Crowd
`crowd_specs()`: seeded background people (8 men, 8 women by default; raise the counts for bigger crowds), varied
skin (Kraków 1795: mostly Polish, with Jewish, German, Ruthenian, Armenian and Hungarian residents), age, build,
hair style and colour, cloth colours, hats. 1024 px textures.

## Animals
dog_vizsla, dog_pomeranian (pets of the well-off), cat (on stall counters), horse (carts, riders), goat, pig, goose,
pigeon (the Rynek's own), dragon (Smok Wawelski, south-west corner). Placeholder quality: readable at street
distance, to be replaced with sculpted meshes later.

## Known gaps
- Cloth is flat colour; needs woven and worn textures.
- No facial animation, no lip sync; expressions available in MPFB later.
- Animals are lofted primitives, not sculpts.
- Interiors do not exist yet; the district population is placed outdoors.
