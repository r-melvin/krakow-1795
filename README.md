# Kraków 1795

Third-person social-stealth prototype set in Kraków after the Third Partition. You are the figurehead of a
movement to take the city back, one institution and one district at a time, through stealth, rumour,
faction politics and the occasional knife. Godot 4.7, GDScript; everything visible and audible is generated by
scripts (Blender 5.2 + MakeHuman for people, procedural bakes for buildings, numpy for sound).

Design: `docs/GDD.md`. Stealth systems: `docs/STEALTH.md`. Animation: `docs/ANIMATION.md`. Audio: `docs/AUDIO.md`.
Animals and their sources: `docs/ANIMALS.md`. Historical people and sources: `docs/HISTORY.md`.

The game does not soften its period: occupation, public punishment, poverty, drink, prostitution, disease and the
bigotries of the age are shown, and some options are closed to a character by sex, origin, faith or inclination.
A notice before the main menu says so.

## Run
```
godot --path .                                              # play
godot -e --path .                                           # open the editor
godot --headless --path . --quit-after 3000 -- --smoke      # headless test: missions, stealth, kit, city, weather, audio
godot --headless --path . --quit-after 6000 -- --smoke      # ... plus the seven-night campaign smoke
godot --path . -- --smoke --shot=/dir                       # windowed: in-engine screenshots
godot --path . -- --smoke --shot-ui=/dir                    # windowed: every UI screen
```
Options: 4x MSAA and temporal AA, bounced light and volumetric mist, fullscreen, VSync, volume, mouse.
The UI fits 1600x900 and 1920x1080.

## What is in the box
- **Campaign** (`scripts/mission/`, `data/campaign.json`, `data/missions.json`): seven nights, each with two or
  three approaches gated by who you are, from *The Printer's Bundle* to *The Kingpin*, a sandbox assassination
  of the river king with seven methods (poison, cargo hook, fire, riot, guillotine tribunal, a stolen bodyguard's
  coat, pistol) that flips the docks district. Day phases with choices; capture, the cells, trial, checkpoints,
  and succession (a nominated heir or one forced on the movement). Estimated first playthrough ~107 minutes.
- **The Whisper Network** (`scripts/mission/rumours.gd`, `data/rumours.json`): rumours are objects carried by
  named people over a social network; you collect them (overhearing, vendors, urchins, the madam, the bard) and
  plant them (press, ballad seller, crier, forged bills, the madam) to move guards, factions and targets. Omens,
  miracles, curses, relics and prophecies spread fastest among the pious.
- **Factions** (`data/factions.json`, `data/campaign.json`): Church, Salon, Underworld, Street, Guilds, Magnates,
  Austria, Russia, Prussia, with a rivalry matrix, arm's-length trust, grievances and sabotage.
- **Stealth** (`scripts/stealth/`, `data/stealth.json`): visibility from posture, sampled light and crowds;
  footstep noise by surface; two-zone vision cones with head sweeps; hiding spots, lean, benches; alert phases
  (alarm, evasion, caution) with a runner to the Corporal; body dragging; distractions; disguise zones with
  trespass; enforcers who know your face; notoriety and wanted bills; intel into the journal; minimap.
- **Kit and traversal** (`scripts/stealth/kit.gd`, `traversal.gd`, `fp_view.gd`): knife-first close quarters,
  powders, throwables, pickups, vault/mantle/hang/climb, keyhole and listening in first person, lock-picking.
- **The city** (`scripts/city/`, `data/city_layout.json`): the Rynek with the Cloth Hall, St Mary's, the Town Hall
  and St Adalbert's; the walled Old Town with alleys, yards and through-passages; Mały Rynek; the Collegium; the
  Florian Gate and Barbican; Kleparz, Garbary, the Vistula docks, Kazimierz with its synagogues, farmland; Wawel
  on the skyline. Thirty interiors keyed to their trades. Street dressing, vendors and hawkers, window life
  (chimney smoke, slops, cooking), street life (drunks, fights, crime, punishment, the brothel, occupation
  scenes, a bard), riots and fire, weather (snow, blizzard, sleet, thaw that melts the roofs, fog, winter day),
  a dorożka and carts, horses, dogs, cats, birds.
- **People** (`assets/blender/build_characters.py`): 135 MakeHuman figures, no two alike (an audit enforces it),
  with body types by role, era clothing cut from the body with ease and folds, hats, grime by trade, bespoke
  faction leaders (some historical, see `docs/HISTORY.md`), a shared 90-clip animation library.
- **Sound** (`tools/gen_sfx.py`, `scripts/audio/`): 189 synthesised sounds; footsteps, hooves, wheels, animals,
  crowd, wind, bells and the hejnał on the clock.

## Performance
Measured with `godot --path . -- --perf` (a plain night, 40 s settle, 1600x900, MSAA 4x + TAA, SDFGI, SSR,
volumetric mist, all systems on) on a 32-core machine with a 16 GB GPU:

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Frame time | 15 ms (~67 fps) |
| Primitives / draw calls per frame | ~4.0M / ~6.8k |
| Night build (menu to street) | 1.1 s; app start to the street 2.2 s; the outer town streams in over ~9 s |
| RAM / VRAM | 2.4 GB / 3.9 GB (3.2 GB textures, VRAM-compressed) |
| Headless CPU frame | ~7 ms (4.5 ms physics) |

Levers if a machine needs them: Options (bounced light and mist, MSAA, TAA), `data/stealth.json` and
`data/window_life.json` budgets, the shadow budget (`scripts/city/shadow_budget.gd`), and the texture caps in
`tools/import_settings.py`.

## Scripts and commands
Blender generators (`blender -b --python <script> [-- args]`):
| Script | Builds |
|---|---|
| `assets/blender/build_assets.py` | buildings, landmarks, props, street dressing, district sets, farmland, ground slabs; bakes textures on first run (`-- --textures` bake only, `-- --rebake`, `-- --only a,b`, `-- --animals` for the procedural animals and the dragon) |
| `assets/blender/build_interiors.py` | the thirty interior sets |
| `assets/blender/build_vendor_props.py`, `build_window_props.py`, `build_kit_props.py` | vendor wares, window-life props, weapons and tools |
| `assets/blender/build_animals.py` | horse, dogs, cat, birds from the fetched sources (leg-IK gaits), dorożka, cart, coach |
| `assets/blender/build_animations.py` | the shared animation library `anim_library.glb` |
| `assets/blender/build_characters.py` | all figures (`-- <names>`, `-- base|cast|townsfolk|district|npc`, `-- --audit` for the uniqueness report) |
| `assets/blender/render_*.py` | check renders: characters (turnaround, face, walk, clip sheets), lineups, sheets, interiors, animals, animation review |

Tools:
| Script | Does |
|---|---|
| `tools/build_all.sh` | rebuilds everything in stages (`--only layout,assets,animals,interiors,props,third_party,animations,audio,characters,import`, `--no-characters`, `JOBS=n` parallel Blender processes), then imports twice with the texture settings applied between |
| `tools/gen_city_layout.py` | writes `data/city_layout.json` |
| `tools/gen_sfx.py` | synthesises `assets/audio/` (`--demo` writes a mix) |
| `tools/fetch_animals.sh` | downloads the CC0/CC-BY animal sources into `assets/third_party/` |
| `tools/import_settings.py` | sets VRAM compression and size caps on every extracted texture's `.import` |
| `tools/with_gpu.sh`, `tools/with_cpu.sh` | machine-wide locks and core slices so parallel builds and test runs do not fight (one Godot at a time) |
| `tools/refresh_screenshots.sh` | captures every screenshot set from the running game into `docs/screenshots/` and prints the windowed performance line |
| `tools/make_release.sh <tag>` | zips the generated models (three parts) and attaches them to a GitHub release |

## Building from a fresh clone
The generated models (`assets/models/*.glb`, about 2.4 GB) are not in git. Two ways to get them:

**A. Download** the three `krakow-1795-models-<tag>-{world,people-a,people-b}.zip` assets from the latest
[GitHub release](https://github.com/r-melvin/krakow-1795/releases) (GitHub caps one asset at 2 GiB), unzip all
three in the project root (they contain `assets/models/` and `assets/ground/`), then:
```
godot --headless --import --path . && python3 tools/import_settings.py && godot --headless --import --path .
godot --path .
```
The second import applies VRAM compression and size caps; without it the game needs four times the memory.

**B. Rebuild** from the scripts:
1. Install Godot 4.7, Blender 5.2, Python 3 with numpy, and for the people the MPFB2 extension plus the MakeHuman
   system assets pack:
   ```
   blender -b --command extension install-file -r user_default -e mpfb.zip     # from extensions.blender.org
   # then extract makehuman_system_assets_cc0.zip into ~/.config/blender/5.2/extensions/.user/user_default/mpfb/data
   ```
2. `tools/build_all.sh` (about 20 minutes on 32 cores; `--no-characters` for a quick city).
3. `godot --path .`
